“The winners won’t hate wrappers. They’ll master them.”
“It’s just a wrapper” — The most misused phrase in AI right now. Just when I thought the “it’s just a wrapper” noise couldn’t get louder, a16z drops the take I’ve been quietly chewing on for months.

If a lawyer tells you they’ve built a legal AI product, don’t roll your eyes and call it a wrapper.
Ask the real questions instead:
- Does it actually work at scale? 
- What’s the engineering? 
- How have they cracked the compute economics? 
- Is the workflow genuinely better than the old ways? 

Because the difference between a toy and a defensible product almost always sits in that “wrapper.”

Shoutout toand thefor not dismissing the wrapper conversation
the "AI wrapper" critique assumes the wrapper is the easy part

But being the wrapper also includes figuring out:
- getting distribution without paying infinite CAC
- creating an amazing UX that's AI native, not derivative
- building a brand, trust in a very noisy landscape
- creating an ecosystem/community
- generating network effects
- making customer service great
- ... plus pricing, hiring, raising money, and everything else

these are not easy!
